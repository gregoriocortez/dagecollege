DAGE College Programs

Learn skills. Create possibilities.

Explore practical programs designed to develop creativity, confidence and real-world skills that can open doors to employment, entrepreneurship and further growth.

01 — OUR PROGRAMS

Find the skill that speaks to you.

From beauty and creative arts to culinary and digital skills, DAGE provides opportunities for students to learn through practical training and creative exploration.

Cosmetology

Explore the art and science of beauty through hair styling, skincare, makeup, nail care and other professional beauty techniques. Students develop practical and theoretical knowledge alongside important hygiene, safety and professional practices.

Creative & Beauty

Baking & Culinary

Learn the preparation, cooking and presentation of food while developing skills in both culinary arts and baking. Training covers ingredients, techniques, food safety and creative presentation for professional kitchens, bakeries, catering and related businesses.

Culinary Arts

Decor

Develop the creativity and practical skills required to transform spaces and events. Students explore design, arrangement, colour, lighting, accessories and artistic presentation while learning how creative ideas can be turned into practical services.

Design & Events

Computer

Build practical digital skills across computer applications, hardware, software, programming, networking and digital literacy. Students gain tools for modern workplaces, research, creativity, problem-solving and future technology opportunities.

Digital Skills

Public Speaking

Develop confidence, communication and presentation skills. Students learn how to structure speeches, engage audiences, manage stage anxiety and communicate ideas effectively in academic, professional and everyday environments.

Communication

Entrepreneurship

Build the mindset and practical understanding needed to turn skills into opportunities. Entrepreneurship supports students in thinking creatively, identifying opportunities, understanding customers and developing sustainable ideas around their skills.

Business & Growth

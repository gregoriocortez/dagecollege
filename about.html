Discover DAGE College

Where passion becomes purpose.

DAGE College is built around the belief that education should give people more than knowledge. It should give them practical skills, confidence, creativity and the opportunity to build a meaningful future.

01 — ABOUT US

Who are we?

A college born from a desire to turn practical skills into opportunities.

DAGE College was established in 2012 and registered with VETA with registration number REG/NACTVET/0502.

The college began with a strong focus on beauty and cosmetology, growing from a practical desire to help young women and people from challenging environments discover skills that could help them shape their futures.

Today, DAGE brings together practical training and creativity across areas including cosmetology, decor, entrepreneurship, life skills, culinary and bakery studies, and computer education.

At the heart of DAGE is a simple idea: when people are given the opportunity to learn, practice and believe in their abilities, they can transform their dreams into something real.

02 — OUR FOUNDER

The woman behind the vision.

Ms. Genoveva Kiliba

An entrepreneur and visionary, Ms. Genoveva Kiliba founded DAGE College in 2012 with a passion for education, practical skills and community development.

Her journey began with a desire to teach people the practical skills of cosmetology and beauty. What began as hands-on teaching gradually grew into a broader vision for an institution where people could learn, develop their abilities and create opportunities for themselves.

Through DAGE, that vision expanded into arts and crafts, cosmetology, decor, bakery and culinary studies and other areas designed to equip students with useful, practical knowledge.

Founder & Visionary
03 — OUR HISTORY

From a salon to a college.

DAGE College began as an idea rooted in practical experience. People would come to a salon looking for opportunities, but many did not yet have the cosmetology skills required to work in the field.

They were taught hands-on whenever an opportunity arose. When a customer came in, it became another opportunity to teach and develop practical skills.

Over time, more people began coming specifically to learn. What started informally eventually developed into the idea of establishing a college.

Cosmetology became the foundation of DAGE and the first subject taught. From that foundation, the institution gradually expanded into other areas of practical and creative education.

The journey began in the mid-2000s, eventually leading to the opening of the first cosmetology class in 2012, with approximately 25 students in a class.

04 — OUR GOALS

Skills with purpose.

DAGE College focuses on nurturing creativity, preserving traditional skills and encouraging innovation across visual and applied arts.

We aim to provide hands-on education that combines technical proficiency with creative expression, allowing students to explore their abilities through areas such as cosmetology, decor, baking and culinary arts and other practical disciplines.

The college also seeks to create an environment where students can develop critical thinking, creativity, cultural awareness and confidence.

Through connections with communities, artisans and industries, DAGE aims to create opportunities for practical experience, entrepreneurship, sustainable careers and meaningful social contribution.

Ultimately, our goal is to empower students to become skilled professionals, entrepreneurs, creators and contributors to their communities.

05 — OUR DREAM

Think beyond today.

DAGE dreams of becoming a place where creativity, tradition and innovation come together. We envision empowering a new generation of skilled artists, craftspeople and professionals who can shape culture, inspire communities and preserve valuable heritage.

Through collaboration, research and outreach, we aspire to transform passion into purpose and practical skills into lasting impact.

06 — OUR DIRECTION

Where we are going.

Vision

To be a leading institution that celebrates and advances the rich traditions of arts and crafts while fostering innovation, creativity and cultural sustainability.


DAGE envisions a future where artistic expression is valued as a force for education, empowerment and positive societal change.


Through excellence in teaching, research and community engagement, we aim to cultivate skilled, thoughtful and responsible professionals who contribute meaningfully to the creative landscape.

Mission

Our mission is to provide transformative education in arts, crafts and practical disciplines by blending traditional knowledge with contemporary practices.


We are committed to nurturing creativity, craftsmanship and critical thinking in a supportive and inclusive learning environment.


Through hands-on learning, community partnerships and interdisciplinary collaboration, we prepare students to contribute meaningfully to the creative industries and society.
